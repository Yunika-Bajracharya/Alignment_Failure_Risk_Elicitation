**Step 2 — Interaction Analysis**
How do the two techniques interact?
- **Additive protection**: each technique independently reduces frequency by its individual factor
- **Sub-additive protection**: techniques share coverage of the same failure pathways; combined is better than either alone but not multiplicatively so
- **Superadditive protection**: techniques address complementary weaknesses; combined is better than the multiplicative product
- **Negative interaction**: one technique undermines or circumvents the other; combined is worse than expected
